Your personal life dashboard as a PWA (installable on your phone like a real app).


---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Setup Guide

### STEP 1 — Create GitHub Repository

1. Go to https://github.com
2. Click the **"+"** icon (top right) → **"New repository"**
3. Name it: `danush-life-os`
4. Set to **Public**
5. Click **"Create repository"**

---

### STEP 2 — Upload All Files

1. In your new repo, click **"uploading an existing file"**
2. Upload ALL files maintaining the folder structure:
   - `public/index.html`
   - `public/manifest.json`
   - `public/sw.js`
   - `src/index.js`
   - `src/App.jsx`
   - `package.json`
   - `vercel.json`
3. Scroll down, click **"Commit changes"**

> ⚠️ Make sure the folders `public/` and `src/` are created correctly in GitHub

---

### STEP 3 — Deploy on Vercel (Free Hosting)

1. Go to https://vercel.com
2. Click **"Sign Up"** → Choose **"Continue with GitHub"**
3. Allow Vercel to access your GitHub
4. Click **"Add New Project"**
5. Find `danush-life-os` → Click **"Import"**
6. Leave all settings default → Click **"Deploy"**
7. Wait ~2 minutes ⏳
8. Vercel gives you a link like: `https://danush-life-os.vercel.app` 🎉

---

### STEP 4 — Install on Your Phone

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open the Vercel link in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **3 dots menu** (top right)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. App icon appears on your home screen ✅

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open the Vercel link in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share button** (bottom center)
3. Scroll down → Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**
5. App icon appears on your home screen ✅

---

### STEP 5 — Future Updates

Whenever I give you updated code:
1. Go to your GitHub repo
2. Click on `src/App.jsx`
3. Click the **pencil icon** (Edit)
4. Replace all content with new code
5. Click **"Commit changes"**
6. Vercel auto-deploys in ~1 minute
7. Refresh the app on your phone — done! ✅
